Social / Live Engagement · Concept

Battle Rooms — merging fan chat rooms for live events

Increase usage, interaction and excitement by linking real-world rivalry into the product.

The Opportunity

Creators and companies already have dedicated fan chat rooms (e.g. Donald Trump room, FC Barcelona room). During high-stakes live events — debates, matches, competitions — fans want more than passive watching. They want rivalry, real-time reaction, and community energy.

The Concept

Temporarily merge rival fan chat rooms into “Battle Rooms” during live events. Example: Trump vs Harris debate → the two political chat rooms fuse into a moderated battle space. Or Barcelona vs Real Madrid → the two fan rooms become a live battle arena for the match.

Like TikTok Live battles, but built around existing fan communities rather than individual streamers. The rooms separate again after the event ends.

Why it works

  • Turns real-world events into high-engagement virtual experiences
  • Leverages existing communities instead of forcing new ones
  • Creates FOMO and appointment viewing for live moments
  • Gives fans a reason to stay longer and interact more
  • Opens new monetization paths (sponsored battles, premium battle rooms, highlights)

Success metrics

Session duration during events, messages per user, retention of battle participants, creator/company adoption of chat rooms, and post-event return rate.

Enterprise · Hardware + AI

BlackBerry AI-Powered Business Device

Re-entering the smartphone market with a productivity-first AI device for professionals.

Problem Statement

BlackBerry is on a mission to re-enter the smartphone market and challenge tech giants like Samsung. The challenge is to create a product that stands out by offering unique value to business professionals, tech enthusiasts, and corporate organizations — reclaiming meaningful market share within the first year.

Market Opportunity

While the smartphone market is dominated by consumer-focused players, a clear gap exists for devices purpose-built for productivity. Companies increasingly need tools that streamline communication, task management, and client interaction. BlackBerry’s heritage as a business-oriented brand positions it perfectly to own this space.

Product Overview

Functionality over aesthetics. An AI-driven operating system deeply embedded to act as a virtual assistant for scheduling, reminders, client communication, task tracking, and workflow management. Predictive responses based on previous interactions, smart calendar optimization, and seamless integration with existing business tools.

Competitive Differentiation

Samsung’s foldable AI devices target tech enthusiasts and entertainment. BlackBerry targets the high-value professional segment that prioritizes work productivity. Specialized AI for task management, workflow automation, and business communication becomes the competitive moat.

Value Proposition

  • Enhanced scheduling & intelligent reminders
  • Client & task management simplification
  • Workplace connectivity as a communication hub
  • Future-proof ecosystem (auto-connect with earbuds, laptops, smartwatches)

Go-to-Market

Positioned as a “work-first” solution. Pilot programs with SMBs for early feedback, direct enterprise sales, and telecom partnerships. Marketing focuses on productivity gains rather than luxury design.

Expected Impact

Increased market share in the professional segment, high retention through workflow dependency, frequent AI feature usage as a success signal, and long-term revenue growth via the expanding ecosystem.
